📊 KEY METRIC FORMULAS

Email CTR %         = Clicks ÷ Opens × 100
Email Revenue       = Clicks × Conversion% × $45
Video Engagement %  = (Likes+Comments+Shares) ÷ Views × 100
Video Revenue       = Views × 2% click × 1% conversion × $50
Signal Win Rate %   = Profitable Trades ÷ Total Signals × 100
Signal Revenue      = Profitable Trades × $200
Daily Revenue Total = Email$ + Video$ + Signals$
