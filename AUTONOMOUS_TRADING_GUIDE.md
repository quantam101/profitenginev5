## Part 2: The 11-Strategy Engine

ProfitEngine uses 11 independent trading strategies:

### 1. **Momentum Strategy**
- Identifies fast-moving trends
- Buys stocks with positive momentum
- Sells when momentum reverses
- Best for: Volatile markets

### 2. **Mean Reversion**
- Buys oversold assets
- Sells overbought assets
- Profits from price normalization
- Best for: Range-bound markets

### 3. **Breakout Strategy**
- Enters when price breaks key levels
- Follows momentum out of consolidation
- Manages risk with tight stops
- Best for: Trending markets

### 4. **Sentiment Analysis**
- Analyzes news sentiment in real-time
- Buys positive sentiment
- Shorts negative sentiment
- Best for: News-driven moves

### 5. **Correlation Detection**
- Identifies price relationships between assets
- Exploits correlation breakdown
- Hedges correlated positions
- Best for: Multi-asset portfolios

### 6. **Technical Pattern Recognition**
- Recognizes chart patterns (head & shoulders, triangles, etc.)
- Enters/exits on pattern confirmation
- High probability setups
- Best for: Consistent patterns

### 7. **Volume Profile**
- Analyzes trading volume at each price level
- Identifies support/resistance zones
- Enters where volume is strong
- Best for: Institutional level trading

### 8. **Machine Learning Prediction**
- Trains on historical price data
- Predicts next-period price movement
- Adapts to changing market conditions
- Best for: Long-term accuracy

### 9. **Microstructure Signal**
- Analyzes bid-ask spreads
- Detects large order flow
- Identifies institutional positioning
- Best for: Intraday trading

### 10. **Risk-Adjusted Return**
- Calculates Sharpe ratio for each trade
- Only enters high-probability trades
- Maximizes risk-adjusted returns
- Best for: Consistent performance

### 11. **Correlation-Based Hedging**
- Monitors correlation between positions
- Adds hedging when correlation increases
- Reduces portfolio drawdown
- Best for: Downside protection

## Part 3: The Risk Management System

### The 6 Gates That Protect Your Capital

**Gate 1: Position Size Limit**
- Maximum position: 5% of account
- Prevents over-concentration
- Ensures risk is manageable

**Gate 2: Daily Loss Limit**
- Maximum daily loss: 2% of account
- Stops trading after limit hit
- Prevents catastrophic days

**Gate 3: Drawdown Protection**
- Maximum drawdown: 10% from peak
- Automatically closes positions
- Protects capital

**Gate 4: Correlation Check**
- Won't add correlated positions
- Ensures diversification
- Reduces systemic risk

**Gate 5: Volatility Adjustment**
- Reduces position size in high volatility
- Increases position size in low volatility
- Adapts to market conditions

**Gate 6: Liquidity Verification**
- Only trades liquid assets
- Ensures easy entry/exit
- Prevents slippage losses
