## 🎬 RECORDING TIPS

**Setup:**
- Quiet room (bedroom, office, or closet works)
- Lamp or window for lighting
- Headphones in (better audio capture)
- Phone on silent

**During:**
- Speak as if talking to a friend (not robotic)
- Pause if you make mistakes (you can edit out)
- Show your screen clearly
- Gesture naturally (camera sees hand movements)

**Screen Recording:**
- Mac: QuickTime Player (cmd+space → QuickTime)
- Windows: OBS Studio (free, professional)
- Windows 11: Win+G (built-in, simple)

**One-Take Rule:**
- You don't need perfection
- One natural take beats 10 edited takes
- Viewers prefer authenticity over perfection
- Upload raw if needed
