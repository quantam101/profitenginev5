# Data Policy - Live Data Only ✅

This repository reports **ONLY real, verified trading data**.

## What Gets Reported
✅ **Real trading results** - Actual executed trades with real P&L  
✅ **Live market data** - Real-time market feeds and analysis  
✅ **Verified revenue** - Actual revenue from sales/trading  
✅ **Production metrics** - Real operational metrics  

## What Does NOT Get Reported
❌ **Synthetic backtests** - Historical simulations (backtest data BLOCKED)  
❌ **Mock/test data** - Test environment results  
❌ **Paper trading** - Simulated trading for strategy testing  
❌ **Unverified data** - Data not verified as real  

## Enforcement
- All reports automatically verified for live data only
- Synthetic data is detected and blocked before commit
- Verification gate prevents non-live data from being published
- Policy enforced by: `verify-live-data-only.yml` workflow
