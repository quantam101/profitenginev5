## HOW TO USE CHATGPT EFFECTIVELY

### Key Principles

**1. Be Specific**
- Instead of: "Write marketing copy"
- Use: "Write a subject line for a cold email targeting SaaS founders with AI tools"

**2. Give Context**
- Who is your audience?
- What's your business?
- What's the goal?
- What's the tone?

**3. Provide Examples**
- Share 1-2 examples of output you like
- ChatGPT will match that style

**4. Use Temperature Settings**
- Lower (0.3-0.5): Factual, consistent output
- Medium (0.7): Balanced creativity
- Higher (0.9+): Creative, varied output

**5. Iterate with Follow-ups**
- First prompt: Get initial ideas
- Follow-up 1: "Make it more [specific quality]"
- Follow-up 2: "Generate 5 variations"
- Follow-up 3: "Pick the best one and expand it"

### Pro Tips

**Chain Multiple Prompts:**
Use output from one prompt as input to another. Example:
1. Generate blog title ideas → Pick best one
2. Use best title in outline prompt → Get structured outline
3. Use outline in writing prompt → Get full draft

**Save Your Templates:**
Create a document with your best prompts. Customize them for each use case.

**Use System Messages:**
Start with: "You are a [role]. Your goal is to [goal]. Always [constraint]."
Then ask your question.

**Test Before Using:**
Always review ChatGPT output. It can:
- Hallucinate facts
- Get outdated info
- Make assumptions
- Produce generic content

**Add Your Data:**
Paste your actual company info, product details, target audience data. More specific = better output.
