## CONTINGENCY SCENARIOS

### Scenario A: Low Open Rate (<30% by Day 5)
- **Cause**: Subject lines not compelling, emails hitting spam
- **Action**: 
  - Check spam folder (ensure legitimacy)
  - Refresh subject lines for Day 6-7 follow-ups
  - A/B test: Send 2-3 variants to non-openers with different subject lines
  - Example: "Partnership" → "Integration Opportunity" → "Quick Question"

### Scenario B: Few Replies (<15% by Day 7)
- **Cause**: Messaging not resonating, audience not right time
- **Action**: 
  - Reduce outreach frequency (wait until Day 10 to re-engage)
  - Test different CTA: "20-min call" → "product demo" → "one-sheet review"
  - Identify 3-4 warm intros to elevate Tier 2-3 opens

### Scenario C: Few Calls Scheduled (<2 by Day 7)
- **Cause**: Timing misalignment, decision-maker availability
- **Action**: 
  - Pick top 5 warm leads
  - Call them directly (phone, not email) if email bounced
  - Propose very specific times: "Tuesday 2-3 PM PT or Wednesday 10-11 AM?"
  - Offer flexibility: "Whatever works for you"

### Scenario D: Tier 3 Shows No Interest
- **Cause**: C-level execs less likely to open cold emails
- **Action**: 
  - Pause Tier 3 cold outreach after Day 7
  - Use Week 2-3 calls with Tier 1-2 to gather case studies
  - Re-approach Tier 3 in July 15-20 with warm intros from early wins
  - Example: "Zapier just partnered with us. Could I introduce you to their team?"

### Scenario E: One Hot Lead Wants to Move Fast
- **Cause**: Company has budget approved + urgent need
- **Action**: 
  - Prioritize them for Week 2 (before scheduled calls)
  - Offer expedited contract review (24-48 hour turnaround)
  - Assign VP Marketing to lead relationship
  - Create case study tracking from day 1

## WEEK 2-3 CALL STRATEGY (June 30 - July 11)

### Pre-Call (15 min before)
- [ ] Review company research + contact background
- [ ] Have one-sheet PDF open + ready to share
- [ ] Dial in 5 minutes early
- [ ] Have call notes template open

### During Call (15 minutes)
- **Intro (1 min)**: "Thanks for taking time. Quick agenda: your priorities, how we help, partnership options, next steps."
- **Discovery (4 min)**: "What's your main goal for partnerships? Who else should be involved? What's your timeline?"
- **Pitch (5 min)**: "Based on what you shared, Tier [X] seems like the best fit. Here's pricing + expected ROI."
- **Close (3 min)**: "Does this make sense? Next step: I'll send a contract for review. Timeline?"
- **Backup (1 min)**: "If not ready now, can I follow up in 2 weeks?"

### Post-Call (5 min after)
- [ ] Log call notes (decision maker, budget, timeline, objections)
- [ ] Send promised materials (one-sheet, contract, case study)
- [ ] Schedule next call (if needed)
- [ ] Update master tracking spreadsheet
