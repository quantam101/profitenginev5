SECTION 4: HOW TO GENERATE MORE PROMPTS
========================================

Notice the pattern above? Every prompt follows a similar structure:

1. GREETING
2. ACKNOWLEDGE THE SITUATION
3. PRESENT THE SOLUTION
4. SHOW THE BENEFITS
5. PROVIDE SPECIFIC NEXT STEPS
6. SIGN OFF

USE THIS FRAMEWORK:

For any business situation you want to automate:

1. Identify the context: "[CUSTOMER_SITUATION]"
2. Add personalization: "[CUSTOMER_NAME]" + "[SPECIFIC_DETAIL]"
3. Build the value: "Here's what matters..." + "Here's what you get..."
4. Include proof: "[CUSTOMER_RESULT]" or "[METRIC]"
5. Create urgency (if appropriate): "[DEADLINE]" or "[LIMITED_OFFER]"
6. Clear CTA: "[SPECIFIC_ACTION]" by "[DATE/TIME]"

You now have a framework to generate unlimited prompts for your specific business.

The 50 prompts above are templates. Adapt them to YOUR business, YOUR customers, YOUR results.
