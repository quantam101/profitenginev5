PLATFORM-SPECIFIC FORMULAS
============================

INSTAGRAM FORMULA:
Hook → Pain → Solution → Proof → CTA

HOOK OPTIONS:
"Stop doing [X], start doing [Y]"
"I wish someone told me [INSIGHT] years ago"
"[NUMBER] people make this mistake"
"This changed my life..."

EXAMPLE:
"Stop posting randomly, start posting strategically.

Random posts = random results.

Strategic posting = consistent growth.

Post when your audience is active.
Post what they actually want.
Post with intention.

That's the difference between 100 and 10K followers.

Which will you choose?"

---

TIKTOK FORMULA (First 3 seconds = Hook):
0-3 sec: HOOK (must stop scroll)
3-15 sec: CONTENT/PAYOFF
15-20 sec: CTA (like/follow/comment)

HOOK OPTIONS:
"You're doing [THING] wrong"
"[NUMBER] ways to [BENEFIT]"
"POV: [PERSPECTIVE]"
"[CONTROVERSIAL] opinion..."
"This saved me..."

---

LINKEDIN FORMULA (Professional):
Observation → Story → Lesson → CTA

EXAMPLE:
"I noticed something at the coffee shop this morning...

[SHORT STORY]

This taught me [LESSON] about [BUSINESS_TOPIC]

The takeaway for you: [ACTIONABLE_INSIGHT]

Want to discuss? Comment below."

---

FACEBOOK FORMULA (Community):
Personal Opening → Story → Relatable Element → Ask for Engagement

EXAMPLE:
"You know what frustrates me?
[PERSONAL_RANT]

I see this with clients all the time...
[STORY]

Can you relate? What frustrates YOU?
Comment below. Let's talk about it."

BATCH CONTENT CREATION GUIDE
=============================

CREATE 28 DAYS OF CONTENT IN 4 HOURS:

SETUP (30 min):
☐ Clean background
☐ Lighting ready
☐ Phone on tripod
☐ Props prepared
☐ Outfits ready

FILMING (2 hours):
☐ Film all talking-head videos (multiple takes)
☐ Get B-roll footage
☐ Testimonial interviews
☐ Alternative angles

EDITING (1-1.5 hours):
☐ Cut videos
☐ Add text/captions
☐ Add music
☐ Optimize for each platform

SCHEDULING (30 min):
☐ Upload to scheduling tool
☐ Set posting times
☐ Add captions/hashtags
☐ Pre-write comments to post

RESULT: 28 Instagram posts + 28 TikTok videos + 28 LinkedIn posts + 28 Facebook posts = 112 pieces created in one day
