SECTION 9: HOW TO GENERATE INFINITE DESIGNS
===========================================

Notice the pattern? Every design follows:

1. CHOOSE A NICHE (gaming, coffee, mental health, etc.)
2. PICK AN EMOTION (funny, motivational, cute, dark humor)
3. ADD SPECIFIC DETAILS (references, puns, inside jokes)
4. APPLY A STYLE (retro, minimalist, watercolor, etc.)
5. OPTIMIZE FOR PRINTING (high contrast, readable text)

FORMULA:
[NICHE] + [EMOTION] + [SPECIFIC_DETAIL] + [STYLE] = DESIGN

Example:
[COFFEE LOVERS] + [FUNNY] + [Morning routine joke] + [MINIMALIST] = Bestseller

You now have the framework to generate unlimited designs.

The 100 prompts above are templates. Combine them, remix them, adapt them to trending niches.

That's how you discover bestsellers.
