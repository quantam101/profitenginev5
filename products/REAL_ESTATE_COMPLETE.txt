SECTION 4: OBJECTION HANDLING SCRIPTS (8 Objections)
===================================================

OBJECTION 1: "I need to think about it"
Script: "Absolutely, that makes sense. Quick question - is it the home that needs thought, or the decision to buy in general? Because if it's the home, I can show you [SIMILAR_OPTION]. If it's the move itself, let's talk about what's making you hesitate. I've helped [NUMBER] families work through this exact thing."

OBJECTION 2: "It's too expensive"
Script: "I hear you. Let me put this in perspective: You're currently paying $[RENT]/month in rent. A mortgage here would be $[PAYMENT]/month - which is actually $[SAVINGS] cheaper per month. Plus you're building equity instead of paying a landlord. Does that change things?"

OBJECTION 3: "I want to look at other options first"
Script: "Smart thinking. Here's what I suggest: Let's compare this to [OTHER_HOMES]. That way you'll know exactly what you're choosing between. Want me to pull comparables so we can see side-by-side?"

OBJECTION 4: "I need to talk to my spouse/partner"
Script: "Of course. When works best for both of you? I can walk you both through the numbers together. I've helped hundreds of couples work through this decision, and I can usually answer all the questions that come up."

OBJECTION 5: "I don't qualify for a mortgage"
Script: "You might be surprised. Let me connect you with a lender who specializes in [SITUATION]. They can run your numbers - often there are options you don't know about."

OBJECTION 6: "The market might drop"
Script: "[NEIGHBORHOOD] has appreciated [PERCENT]% over [YEARS]. Even if prices dip 5%, you're still ahead in [TIME]. Plus you're locking in your payment for 30 years - rent only goes up."

OBJECTION 7: "I'll wait for prices to drop"
Script: "I understand that logic. But here's what usually happens: Prices don't drop much, rates go up, and homes disappear. By the time people think it's a buyer's market, they're competing with everyone. Want to see historical data?"

OBJECTION 8: "I'm not ready yet"
Script: "Got it. What would make you ready? [SAVINGS]/[CREDIT]/[JOB]? Tell me the timeline and I can help you hit that goal. Many clients didn't think they were ready either."
