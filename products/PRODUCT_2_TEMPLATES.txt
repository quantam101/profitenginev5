## ENGAGEMENT LOOP FORMULAS

### The Comment Bait
Post statement that makes people want to defend/attack/explain

Examples:
"[Controversial opinion most people disagree with]"
"The best [category] is actually [unexpected option]"
"[Popular thing] is overrated. Here's why:"
"Most [type of people] don't realize [statement]"

Formula: Statement they'll feel compelled to correct/debate

### The Question Hook
End EVERY post with question that makes them comment

Good:
"What's the #1 thing holding you back from [goal]?"
"How would you [situation]?"
"Which of these resonates most with your experience?"

Bad:
"What do you think?" (too generic)
"Let me know below" (no reason to reply)

### The CTA Hierarchy
Primary: "Tag someone who needs to see this"
Secondary: "Save this for later"
Tertiary: "Send this to a friend"
Last resort: "Like and comment"

## PLATFORM-SPECIFIC BEST PRACTICES

INSTAGRAM:
- Post 9am or 7pm in your timezone
- Respond to first 10 comments in 1 hour
- Use 20-30 words max in captions (algorithm likes comments)
- Captions should invite discussion

TIKTOK:
- Post 9am or 7pm
- Video should stop scroll by second 2
- Trending sounds = higher reach
- Hashtags are less important than sound + hook

FACEBOOK:
- Post 1pm or 8pm
- Emojis increase engagement 40%
- Questions get 5x more comments than statements
- Video > Image > Text

LINKEDIN:
- Post 8am Tuesday-Thursday
- Genuine vulnerability increases reach 2x
- First 2 lines make or break click-through
- Asking for input = algorithm boost

TWITTER/X:
- Post during business hours (8am-6pm)
- Threads perform better than single tweets
- Retweet worthiness matters more than reach
- Replies > Likes for algorithm

## BATCHING WORKFLOW

**Set aside 1 hour weekly:**

Block 1 (15 min): Plan 4 weeks of themes
- Week 1: Educational
- Week 2: Personal/Story
- Week 3: Strategic/Thought Leadership
- Week 4: Promotional/Offer

Block 2 (30 min): Create content
- Write 12 captions using templates
- Pick 8 images/videos
- Schedule across platforms
- Set hashtags and mentions

Block 3 (15 min): Engagement prep
- Set reminder to respond to comments
- Prepare 3-5 response templates
- Note trending sounds for TikTok

**Result: 4 weeks of content created in 1 hour**
