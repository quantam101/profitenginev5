DAY 29 (MONDAY): The Story Framework That Converts
"Everyone thinks they need to tell better stories.

Actually, you need to tell stories in a better way.

Here's the framework:

SETUP: 'This is me 2 years ago...' (The before state)
STRUGGLE: 'I tried everything...' (The pain they're in)
SHIFT: 'Then I realized...' (The turning point)
SOLUTION: 'Here's exactly what I did...' (The framework)
RESULT: 'Here's what happened...' (The proof it works)
TEACHING: 'Here's what this means for you...' (How they apply it)
CTA: 'Try this...' (The one action to take)

This framework works because it takes people on a journey.

They see themselves in your BEFORE.
They feel validated in your STRUGGLE.
They get hope from your SHIFT.
They get actionable steps in your SOLUTION.
They believe it's possible from your RESULT.
They know how to apply it from your TEACHING.
They know what to do next from your CTA.

Master this framework and you'll never run out of ideas."

DAY 30 (TUESDAY): Behind-the-Scenes - Filming a Story
"This is what it looks like when I film a story-based video:

First, I write the story. (Takes 10 minutes)

I don't script the whole thing. I just outline:
- The before state
- One key struggle
- The turning point
- What I did about it
- What happened
- One lesson for them

Then I hit record. (Takes 15 minutes)

I don't do multiple takes. One take. Raw. Real.

That one take captures:
- Real emotion
- Real pauses
- Real 'ums' and 'ahs'
- Real human-ness

Then I edit minimally. (Takes 15 minutes)

I add text overlays on the key insights so people understand.

I add captions so it works on mute.

I add music that matches the mood.

That's it. 40 minutes total.

And that 40 minutes generates more engagement than 4 hours on 'polished' content.

Because people respond to realness, not production value."

BATCH CONTENT CREATION GUIDE - COMPLETE SYSTEM
================================================================================

CREATE 30 DAYS OF CONTENT IN ONE AFTERNOON

The Goal: In 4 hours, create enough content for 1 full month

Why This Works: Instead of creating 1 post per day (30 hours), you create all 30 at once (4 hours). The efficiency comes from batching.

TIMELINE: 4 HOURS TOTAL

HOUR 1 (0:00-1:00): Setup and Planning (60 minutes)

TIME: 0:00-0:15 (15 minutes)
TASK: Physical Setup
☐ Clean background (or choose filming location)
☐ Set up lighting (window, ring light, or desk lamp)
☐ Position phone on tripod
☐ Test audio (audio quality matters)
☐ Have props nearby if needed
☐ Wear outfit(s) - consider doing multiple looks to create variety

TIME: 0:15-0:30 (15 minutes)
TASK: Mental Preparation
☐ Review your themes for the week (what are you teaching?)
☐ Brainstorm 3-5 core ideas that will become all 30 posts
☐ Write 1-2 sentence outline for each idea
☐ Clear your mind - no rushing

TIME: 0:30-1:00 (30 minutes)
TASK: Script Outlines (Light, Not Heavy)
Don't write full scripts. Just bullets.

Example outline for "Why Systems Matter":
- Hook: 'Systems beat motivation'
- Explain why
- Give one example
- CTA: 'What system do you need?'

Create 5 outline variations on this theme. That's your month.

HOUR 2 (1:00-2:00): Filming (60 minutes)

TIME: 1:00-1:15 (15 minutes)
TASK: Film Talking Head Videos
Hit record. Use your outline. Don't overthink. One take is fine.
Goal: 5-8 videos (some for different platforms, some for reposting)

TIME: 1:15-1:30 (15 minutes)
TASK: B-Roll and Supporting Footage
Film yourself working, writing, showing examples
This becomes your video intros/outros

TIME: 1:30-1:45 (15 minutes)
TASK: If Using Photos
Take 20-30 photos
Different angles, different expressions, different props

TIME: 1:45-2:00 (15 minutes)
TASK: Testimonials/Alternative Content
If you have a camera person: Film testimonials of happy customers/students
If you're solo: Prepare graphics, templates, or alternate content that needs no filming

HOUR 3 (2:00-3:00): Editing (60 minutes)

TIME: 2:00-2:15 (15 minutes)
TASK: Organize All Footage
Label your videos
Create 3 folders: 'To Edit', 'Reels', 'Stories'

TIME: 2:15-2:45 (30 minutes)
TASK: Edit Videos
Use: CapCut, Adobe Premiere, or Descript
For each video:
☐ Cut out pauses and 'ums'
☐ Add text overlays on key points
☐ Add captions (required for mute viewing)
☐ Add music (royalty-free from YouTube, Epidemic Sound, Artlist)
☐ Optimize dimensions for each platform

TIME: 2:45-3:00 (15 minutes)
TASK: Create Graphics
Use Canva for quote graphics, carousels, story templates
Create 10-15 different graphics from your content themes

HOUR 4 (3:00-4:00): Scheduling & Captions (60 minutes)

TIME: 3:00-3:30 (30 minutes)
TASK: Write Captions
Don't rush this. Good captions matter.
For each video:
☐ Hook (first 1-2 lines must stop scroll)
☐ Body (explanation)
☐ CTA (one action)

TIME: 3:30-3:45 (15 minutes)
TASK: Schedule Everything
Use: Later, Buffer, Meta Business Suite, TweetDeck
Set posting times:
- Instagram: 10am and 7pm (test for your audience)
- TikTok: 7am and 7pm
- LinkedIn: 8am
- Facebook: 12pm and 6pm
- Twitter: 9am and 5pm

TIME: 3:45-4:00 (15 minutes)
TASK: Preparation for Week
☐ Pre-write 5 comments to leave on your own posts (to kick off engagement)
☐ Prepare hashtag sets for each platform
☐ Create a backup folder with extra content
☐ Take a screenshot of the schedule so you know what's posted when

FINAL RESULT:

☐ 30 videos (various platforms)
☐ 15-20 static posts (carousels, quotes, images)
☐ 10-15 graphics
☐ All captions written and scheduled
☐ All content optimized per platform
☐ 1 full month of content from 4 hours
