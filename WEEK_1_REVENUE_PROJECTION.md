## ⚡ What Affects Week 1 Revenue

### Positive Factors (Push sales UP)
- ✅ Existing audience (followers, email list)
- ✅ Social media promotion
- ✅ Pre-launch buzz/teasing
- ✅ Etsy shop age (mature shops rank higher)
- ✅ Product relevance to trending topics
- ✅ Gumroad creator credibility

### Negative Factors (Push sales DOWN)
- ❌ No social promotion
- ❌ New Etsy shop (takes 2-4 weeks for algo boost)
- ❌ No existing audience
- ❌ Generic product titles/descriptions
- ❌ No customer reviews yet
- ❌ Product not discoverable via search
