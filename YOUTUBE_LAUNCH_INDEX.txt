COMMON QUESTIONS ANSWERED
================================================================================

Q: What if I can't finish all 3 hours in one block?
A: Don't split it. The 3-hour window is optimized for momentum and quality.
   If you can't do it all at once, reschedule for a date when you can.

Q: What if I mess up during recording?
A: Re-record that video. Don't upload a substandard video.
   Quality matters for your first impression.

Q: What if my internet is slow?
A: Upload times may increase, but parallel uploads help.
   See Hour 2 timeline for realistic upload speeds.

Q: What if a video won't schedule?
A: See "TROUBLESHOOTING" in YOUTUBE_LAUNCH_SEQUENCE.md
   Common fix: Verify date is after today, timezone is PT.

Q: What if I publish a video early by accident?
A: Delete it immediately, re-upload on correct schedule.
   See "ROLLBACK / EMERGENCY PROCEDURES" in YOUTUBE_LAUNCH_SEQUENCE.md

Q: Can I change videos after scheduling?
A: Yes! Edit the draft (not published), save, and it updates the scheduled video.

Q: What if engagement is low after launch?
A: See "IF VIEWS/ENGAGEMENT BELOW EXPECTATIONS" in YOUTUBE_LAUNCH_SUCCESS_METRICS.txt
   Contains action steps to boost performance.

Q: What if I need to add more videos later?
A: Follow the same 3-hour playbook for each batch.
