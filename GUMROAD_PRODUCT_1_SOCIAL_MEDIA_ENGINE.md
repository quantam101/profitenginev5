## POST 3: "THE REAL REASON YOUR PROFITABLE STRATEGY BLEW UP"

**Publish**: Wednesday 10:00 AM ET  
**Platform Priority**: Threads (thread format, 5-7 parts)

### THREADS VERSION

Hook:
> Your backtest was perfect. Your live trading lasted 3 weeks. Here's why.

**Part 1/5 — Black Swan Events**:
Your backtest looked at the last 10 years of data. Every trade worked. Every loss was small. Every win was captured.

Then you traded it live for 3 weeks and a black swan event wiped out 6 months of gains.

A black swan is an event so rare it doesn't show up in historical data:
- Flash crash (May 6, 2010: S&P 500 fell 9% in 5 minutes)
- Fed announcement (March 2020: markets halted 4 times)
- Geopolitical shock (Russia invades Ukraine, markets freeze)

Your backtest didn't have these. Your live account did.

**Part 2/5 — Slippage Math**:
Your backtest assumed you'd exit at exactly the price you wanted.

Live slippage math:
- Expected: 4% slippage on entry/exit
- Actual: 12% slippage (3x worse)

Why? 
- Your order moves the market (you're not as small as you think)
- Spread widens during volatility
- Liquidity evaporates when you need it
- Front-running algorithms beat you to the exit

A single trade cost you $5,000 more than expected. Over 20 trades, that's $100,000 in unexpected losses.

**Part 3/5 — Liquidity Constraints**:
Your backtest assumed you could exit whenever you wanted.

Live reality:
- Stock halts (circuit breakers stop trading)
- No bid/ask spread (no one buying at your price)
- Overnight gaps (close at $100, open at $85)
- Illiquidity during crashes (spreads widen 10x)

You wanted out. You couldn't. You held through a 40% drawdown you never expected.

**Part 4/5 — Correlation Breakdown**:
You diversified across 10 stocks to reduce risk.

Backtest correlation: 0.3 (low, good diversification)
Live crash correlation: 0.92 (everything moves together)

Your "diversified" portfolio moved like one asset. You lost on all 10 simultaneously. 2-3x more risk than you calculated.

**Part 5/5 — The ProfitEngine Solution**:
Before I trade any strategy, it passes 5 stress tests:

1. **Black Swan Test**: Replay worst 10 days in market history. Does strategy survive?
2. **Slippage Test**: Assume 3x expected slippage. Still profitable?
3. **Liquidity Test**: What if bid/ask spreads widen 5x? Can you exit?
4. **Correlation Test**: Assume 0.95 correlation across all assets. Still diversified?
5. **Regime Test**: Does strategy work in trending AND mean-reverting markets?

If it fails any, I don't trade it.

Which of these broke your strategy? (All 5 happened to mine once.)
