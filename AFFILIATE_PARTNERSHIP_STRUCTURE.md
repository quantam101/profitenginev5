# Affiliate Partnership Structure — ProfitEngine v5

Complete monetization strategy for trading education content with transparent affiliate integration, FTC compliance, and revenue tracking.

---

## Overview

**Revenue Model**: Multi-tier affiliate partnerships across trading platforms, educational tools, and complementary products.

**Guiding Principles**:
- Only promote products/platforms used internally and tested
- Transparent disclosure on every affiliate link
- FTC-compliant messaging (no endorsements without disclosure)
- Organic integration into content (educational context, not promotional)
- Track every conversion and optimize placement
- Community trust over short-term revenue

**Expected Annual Revenue**: $8K–$15K (500–1,500 active users, 5–10% conversion, $5–$15 per conversion average)

## FTC Compliance Guidelines

### Legal Requirements (US FTC Act §5)

1. **Disclosure Must Be**:
   - Clear and conspicuous (not buried in fine print)
   - Plain language (no legal jargon)
   - Near the affiliate link (not just at bottom of page)
   - Present BEFORE the user clicks

2. **Prohibited Practices**:
   - ❌ Endorsing products you don't use
   - ❌ False performance claims ("guaranteed $10K/month")
   - ❌ Endorsing without disclosure
   - ❌ Hiding disclosure in abbreviations ("*" without asterisk explanation)

3. **Enforcement Risk**:
   - FTC fines: up to $43,792 per violation (2024)
   - Reputational damage (YouTube/social platforms remove videos)
   - Account suspension (Stripe, PayPal, affiliate networks)

### Best Practices

✅ **DO**:
- Test products before recommending
- Disclose all affiliate relationships
- Use consistent, recognizable disclosure language
- Track which links are earning; audit underperformers
- Update disclosures if partnership changes

❌ **DON'T**:
- Shill products you haven't used
- Hide affiliate relationship in fine print
- Overstate earnings ("I make $10K/month")
- Use affiliate links from people who violated FTC rules
- Recommend competitors if you have exclusive affiliate deal

## Quarterly Review Checklist

**Every 90 days, audit**:

- [ ] Which affiliates are driving revenue? (focus on top 3)
- [ ] Which content pieces are highest-performing? (high CTR + conversion)
- [ ] Are disclosures clear and compliant? (FTC spot check)
- [ ] Commission rates changing? (renegotiate if >$500/month)
- [ ] New affiliate opportunities? (quarterly partner outreach)
- [ ] Competitor partnerships? (adjust positioning)
- [ ] Audience feedback? (what products do readers ask about?)
- [ ] Attribution model still accurate? (cookies, last-click vs. first-click)
