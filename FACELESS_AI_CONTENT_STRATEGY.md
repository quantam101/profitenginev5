## 💰 PAID ADS STRATEGY

### Platform 1: TikTok Ads
**Budget**: $100-300/day
**Best for**: POD, Etsy, Gumroad
**ROAS**: 3:1 to 10:1 (realistic)

**Ad Setup**:
```
1. Create Catalog of 10 best-performing organic videos
2. Run as "Spark Ads" (promote your own videos)
3. Target: Creators (interest), Entrepreneurs, Etsy users
4. Bid: $0.50-2.00 CPC
5. Convert: Landing page with email capture
```

**Example Campaign**:
- **Video**: "I Made $5K on Etsy" (HeyGen)
- **Audience**: Ages 18-45, interested in side hustles
- **Copy**: "This creator made $5K/month passively. Here's how..."
- **Landing**: Lead capture → Email sequence → Gumroad/Services
- **Budget**: $100/day
- **Expected**: 500+ views → 50-100 clicks → 2-5 email signups → 1 sale
- **Revenue**: $29.99-99.99 (Gumroad) or $500+ (service)

---

### Platform 2: Google Ads (Search)
**Budget**: $50-150/day
**Best for**: High-intent (people searching for "how to")
**ROAS**: 5:1 to 15:1 (best ROI)

**Ad Setup**:
```
Search keywords:
- "how to make money on etsy"
- "printify setup guide"
- "gumroad passive income"
- "amazon kdp publishing"
- "pod dropshipping tutorial"
- "content automation tools"

Ad copy: "Make $500/mo with POD. [Your Guide]. Trusted by 1000+ creators."
Landing page: Blog post with CTA → Email list / Product links
```

**Keyword Research**:
- Use Google Keyword Planner (free, in Google Ads)
- Search volume: 1,000+ monthly searches
- CPC: $0.50-3.00
- Target long-tail: "how to make money etsy 2025"

**Example Campaign**:
- **Keyword**: "how to make money etsy" (500 searches/month, $1.50 CPC)
- **Budget**: $50/day
- **Expected**: 33 clicks → 10% conversion → 3 signups → 1 sale
- **Revenue**: $50-500 per day

---

### Platform 3: Facebook/Instagram Ads
**Budget**: $50-200/day

**Ad Setup**:
