METRICS DISPLAYED
=================

Summary Cards (4 KPIs):
  1. Active Traders Today        [count]
  2. Total Trades Today          [count]
  3. Daily P&L Total             [$USD]
  4. Avg P&L per Trade           [$USD]

Leaderboard Tables (3):
  1. Top 5 Performers (Win Rate)
     Columns: Rank, Trader, Win %, P&L, Trades
  2. Top 5 Biggest Gainers (P&L %)
     Columns: Rank, Trader, Gain %, P&L, Trades
  3. Upsell Candidates (Premium Ready)
     Columns: Trader, P&L, Win %, Trades, Days, Urgency

Total Data Points: 30+ metrics aggregated from clients.db

=====================

  RUNTIME_API_URL     = https://your-runtime-domain.com
                        (or http://runtime:8080 for local dev)
  CLIENT_API_KEY      = your-actual-api-key-here

Optional (default to localhost):
  CLIENTS_DB_PATH     = /path/to/clients.db
