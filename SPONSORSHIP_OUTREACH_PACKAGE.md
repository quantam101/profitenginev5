ProfitEngine v5 is an **enterprise affiliate marketing intelligence platform** serving 500+ active traders and entrepreneurs. With 150K+ monthly impressions across YouTube, TikTok, and LinkedIn, we offer sponsorship partners:

- **Audience**: High-intent business owners, affiliate marketers, traders, SaaS operators

- **ROI**: 3-5x ROI documented across previous integrations (affiliate revenue + direct customers)

- Custom feature development (10-20 hours scoped)

- Revenue share on dedicated landing page (25-35% of referred deals)

## Key Metrics for Sponsors

All partnerships include quarterly reporting on:

| Metric | Definition | Benchmark |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| **Impressions/Month** | Total ad/content views (email, video, landing page, social) | 50K–150K |
| **Click-Through Rate** | % of impressions leading to sponsor landing page | 3–8% (B2B) |
| **Conversion Rate** | % of clicks converting to trials/sign-ups | 5–15% |
| **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)** | Average revenue per referred customer (Y1) | $500–$3,000 |
| **ROI for Sponsor** | (Revenue generated / sponsorship cost) | 3–5x typical |
| **Affiliate Commission Share** | % of referred revenue sponsor keeps (Tier 2+) | 15–35% |

## Exclusions & Red Flags

Do NOT contact:
- Competitors: Refersion, Impact, Kenshoo, Singular (direct attribution competitors)
- Predatory platforms: MLM software, spam email providers, scam affiliate networks
- Declining/saturated: Quora (declining traffic), Medium (algorithm throttled creator revenue)
- Legal concerns: No crypto platforms, no gambling, no pharmaceutical ads
