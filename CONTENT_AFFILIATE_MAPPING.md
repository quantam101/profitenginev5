# Content-Affiliate Mapping & Revenue Projections

Comprehensive guide linking content pieces to affiliate partners, tracking conversions, and projecting revenue based on audience size and engagement.

---

## Overview

**Goal**: Every blog post, video, email, and social message should recommend relevant affiliate partners in a way that feels natural and educational.

**Content → Audience → Affiliate → Revenue Funnel**:

```
Writer creates content
    ↓
Audience finds content (organic, email, social)
    ↓
Reader sees relevant affiliate link (contextual placement)
    ↓
Reader clicks affiliate link
    ↓
Reader signs up / purchases with partner
    ↓
Affiliate receives commission
    ↓
ProfitEngine receives payment
```

## Optimization Opportunities

### 1. High-Traffic, Low-Conversion Content

**Identify**: 
```sql
SELECT source, COUNT(*) as clicks, SUM(conversions) as conversions
FROM affiliate_events
WHERE timestamp >= NOW() - INTERVAL '30 days'
GROUP BY source
HAVING COUNT(*) > 50 AND SUM(conversions) / COUNT(*) < 0.03;
```

**Action**: Test new affiliate link placement, messaging, or partner

**Example**: "Setup Guide" gets 500 clicks/month, only 2% conversion
- Current: $25/month
- If we improve to 4% conversion: $50/month (+100% revenue)
- How: Move IBKR link higher, test different CTA

### 2. High-Conversion, Low-Traffic Content

**Identify**: 
```sql
SELECT source, COUNT(*) as clicks, ROUND(SUM(conversions)::numeric / COUNT(*), 3) as conv_rate
FROM affiliate_events
WHERE timestamp >= NOW() - INTERVAL '30 days'
GROUP BY source
HAVING COUNT(*) < 30 AND SUM(conversions) / COUNT(*) > 0.10;
```

**Action**: Promote content more (repurpose for social, email, internal links)

**Example**: "Options Greeks Deep Dive" video has 50 clicks, 15% conversion
- Current: $375/month (15 conversions × $25)
- If we 3x traffic: $1,125/month
- How: Add to newsletter, share on Twitter, link from related posts

### 3. Right Content, Wrong Affiliate

**Identify**: Content with decent traffic but low revenue per click

**Action**: Test different affiliate partner for same content

**Example**: Psychology blog post has 300 clicks but converting to Udemy at 2%
- Current: $9/month
- If we feature Gumroad instead (5% conversion): $30/month (+233%)
- How: Reposition as "get my psychology workbook" instead of "take a course"

## Best Practices for Content Affiliate Integration

### DO:

✅ **Recommend only what you use**: Have you actually used Polygon.io? If not, don't promote it.

✅ **Disclose clearly**: Every affiliate link gets "Affiliate Link" badge or explicit disclosure.

✅ **Provide value first**: Teach the strategy completely before the CTA. Then mention platform.

✅ **Test positioning**: Try link in intro, middle, and bottom. Track which converts best.

✅ **Update content regularly**: Content from 6 months ago has broken links? Fix them.

✅ **Link to specific affiliate URLs**: Use unique tracking codes. Know which link performs.

✅ **Optimize for mobile**: 50% of traffic is mobile. Affiliate links must be mobile-friendly.

### DON'T:

❌ **Shill products you don't use**: "I highly recommend X" when you've never used it destroys trust.

❌ **Hide affiliates in fine print**: Disclose above the fold, near the link.

❌ **Overload with links**: 1–3 affiliate links per article max. More = spammy.

❌ **Link to generic homepages**: Link directly to signup page or specific offer (not amazon.com, but amazon.com/s?k=trading+book)

❌ **Use old, expired links**: Test links monthly. Check for broken affiliate redirects.

❌ **Ignore mobile**: No affiliate links work on mobile? You're leaving 50% of revenue on table.
