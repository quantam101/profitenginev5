## TROUBLESHOOTING

**Problem: "Gumroad says I'm not a verified seller"**
Solution: 
- Go to app.gumroad.com/edit
- Click "Verify account"
- Complete verification (email or ID)
- Wait 24-48 hours for approval

**Problem: "Files aren't uploading"**
Solution:
- Make sure file size is under 5GB
- PDFs work best (avoid complex formats)
- Try a different file format (ZIP instead of individual files)

**Problem: "Not getting sales"**
Solution:
- Expand email list (ask for more people to promote to)
- Create blog content about products
- Post on Twitter/LinkedIn daily
- Ask customers for testimonials
- Create YouTube video reviewing the bundle

**Problem: "Stripe not connected"**
Solution:
- Go to app.gumroad.com/dashboard/settings
- Click "Change payment method"
- Sign in to Stripe or create account
- Complete bank account verification
