## Brand Voice Consistency

### Your Voice (Based on Existing Content)

**Tone**:
- Data-driven (always include numbers)
- Vulnerable (share losses, mistakes, lessons learned)
- Technical depth (understand the mechanisms, not just patterns)
- Practical (everything should be actionable)
- Skeptical (question consensus, show data)
- Educational (explain why, not just what)

**What to Avoid**:
- Hype ("This strategy is revolutionary!")
- Certainty ("This will definitely make money")
- Complexity without explanation ("Just use the Kelly Criterion")
- Generic advice ("Do your research")
- Fake enthusiasm ("🚀 🚀 🚀")
- Overselling ("Join 10,000 traders")

**Guardrails**:
- Always explain the downside (every strategy fails sometimes)
- Always include your personal experience (relatable)
- Always show the math (credible)
- Always acknowledge risk (honest)
- Always invite debate (community-building)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Over-Selling ProfitEngine
**What happens**: People see through it, engagement drops  
**Fix**: Mention ProfitEngine only when genuinely relevant (once per 5-10 posts)  
**Rule**: 95% pure value, 5% soft product mentions

### Mistake 2: Inconsistent Posting
**What happens**: Algorithm deprioritizes you, growth stalls  
**Fix**: Commit to 3-5 posts/week and stick to it  
**Rule**: Schedule 2 weeks ahead to ensure consistency

### Mistake 3: Ignoring Replies
**What happens**: Engagement dies, algorithm buries posts  
**Fix**: Reply to 100% of comments in first 2 hours, 70% in hours 2-12  
**Rule**: Set phone reminder to engage right after posting

### Mistake 4: Only Promoting to Your Own Audience
**What happens**: Growth plateaus, echo chamber  
**Fix**: Engage with 50-100 other traders' content weekly  
**Rule**: Follow 20-30 new traders/week, engage with 3-5 posts each

### Mistake 5: Using Low-Quality Graphics
**What happens**: Posts look amateur, hurt credibility  
**Fix**: Use 1-2 high-quality graphics per post (screenshot + design)  
**Rule**: Templates > original designs (consistency, speed)

### Mistake 6: Posting Fake or Uncertain Results
**What happens**: People test it, it fails, credibility destroyed  
**Fix**: Only share backtests you've stress-tested, real results only  
**Rule**: Never claim results you haven't verified on multiple timeframes

### Mistake 7: No Clear CTA
**What happens**: Engagement without conversion  
**Fix**: Every post should have a clear engagement ask (question or action)  
**Rule**: "Reply with..." > "What do you think?" (be specific)
