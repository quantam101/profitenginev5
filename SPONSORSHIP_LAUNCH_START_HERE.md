## CONTINGENCY QUICK REFERENCE

**If open rate < 30%:**
→ Refresh subject lines for Day 6-7 follow-ups. A/B test new angles.

**If reply rate < 15%:**
→ Test different CTAs (call vs. demo vs. question). Reduce frequency.

**If <2 calls scheduled by Day 7:**
→ Call top 5 leads directly (phone outreach). Propose very specific times.

**If Tier 3 no response:**
→ Pause cold Tier 3. Use Tier 1-2 wins to warm-intro Tier 3 in July 15-20.

**Full contingency guide** → See `SPONSORSHIP_LAUNCH_SUMMARY.md`

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **❌ Generic emails**: "Your customers need..." → **✓ Specific**: "Slack users managing affiliate campaigns need..."
2. **❌ Placeholders left in emails**: "[Company]", "[First Name]" → **✓ Real names**: "Sarah", "Zapier"
3. **❌ Slow response time**: Reply after 8+ hours → **✓ Fast**: Reply within 2 hours
4. **❌ No follow-up plan**: Email sent, assume they'll reply → **✓ Systematic**: Follow-up sequence prepared, Day 6-7 execution ready
5. **❌ Cold Tier 3 only**: Send emails to C-level without warm intro → **✓ Smart**: Use warm intros for OpenAI, Stripe, Mailchimp
