================================================================================
SUPPORT & TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

PROBLEM: Upload fails
SOLUTION:
  1. Go to Studio → Uploads
  2. Click failed video → Delete
  3. Wait 5 minutes
  4. Re-upload with same metadata
  5. Try different browser if persistent

PROBLEM: Schedule doesn't save
SOLUTION:
  1. Verify date is AFTER today
  2. Timezone: Always use PT
  3. Click Schedule again
  4. Verify status changed to "Scheduled"

PROBLEM: Thumbnail won't upload
SOLUTION:
  1. Convert to JPG (if PNG issues)
  2. Verify dimensions: 1280×720px exactly
  3. Verify file size: <2MB
  4. Try different image (save locally)
  5. Clear browser cache

PROBLEM: Views stuck at 0 after 24 hours
SOLUTION:
  1. Verify video is Public (not Scheduled)
  2. Check for content flags (inappropriate, etc.)
  3. Boost social media sharing
  4. Share in relevant communities
  5. Test paid promotion
