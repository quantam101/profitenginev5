## 1. SERVICES PROVIDED

Already Here LLC agrees to provide fleet maintenance and repair services including:

- **Preventive maintenance**: Oil changes, filter replacements, fluid checks, tire rotations
- **Diagnostic services**: Computer diagnostics, brake inspections, electrical diagnostics
- **Repair services**: Engine work, transmission, electrical, suspension, brake service
- **Emergency roadside dispatch**: Towing coordination, roadside assistance
- **Fleet monitoring**: Maintenance scheduling, record-keeping, compliance documentation
- **Parts sourcing**: Bulk discounts, OEM/aftermarket sourcing

---

## 2. PRICING STRUCTURE

### 2.1 Monthly Retainer Fee
- **Base Retainer**: $[1,500-5,000]/month (based on fleet size)
- **Fleet Size Tiers**:
  - 5-10 vehicles: $1,500/month
  - 11-20 vehicles: $2,500/month
  - 21-35 vehicles: $3,500/month
  - 35+ vehicles: $5,000+/month

**Retainer includes**:
- Unlimited diagnostic consultations
- Fleet maintenance scheduling
- Parts markup at cost + 15% (vs. 40-50% retail markup)
- Emergency roadside dispatch coordination
- Compliance documentation and records

### 2.2 Service Charges
- **Per-visit labor rate**: $85/hour (vs. $125-150 retail)
- **Minimum service charge**: $150 per visit
- **Emergency surcharge**: +25% for after-hours/weekend (Saturday morning-Sunday evening)
- **Warranty**: 12 months/12,000 miles on repairs

### 2.3 Volume Discount
- Annual spend $20K+: Additional 5% discount on parts/labor
- Annual spend $35K+: Additional 10% discount on parts/labor

## 3. MISSION COMMITMENT

As a nonprofit/government partner, Already Here LLC commits to:

- **30% cost savings** vs. retail auto repair (average client saves $5,000-$15,000/year)
- **Reinvestment**: 5% of fleet service revenue goes back to [Client Mission] programs
- **Priority scheduling**: 24-hour turnaround for routine maintenance, 48-hour for major repairs
- **Transparent pricing**: All quotes provided in writing before work begins
- **Dedicated account manager**: Single point of contact for all fleet needs

Already Here LLC maintains:

- Monthly fleet health report (compliance status, pending maintenance, cost projections)

## 5. DISPATCH & SCHEDULING

### 5.1 Routine Maintenance
- **Client initiates via**: Phone, email, or fleet dashboard
- **Response time**: 24 hours for confirmation and scheduling
- **Standard turnaround**: 2-5 business days for routine services
- **Waiting pickup**: Available at client location or coordinated towing

### 5.2 Emergency/Breakdown
- **24/7 emergency line**: [phone number]
- **Roadside dispatch**: Towing, lockout service, fuel delivery coordination
- **Emergency parts**: Same-day parts availability for critical repairs
- **Coverage**: Up to $200/incident emergency response fee (waived if service exceeds $500)

### 5.3 Fleet Maintenance Schedule
Client and ProfitEngine establish joint schedule:
- **Preventive maintenance**: Quarterly reviews, annual inspection
- **Oil changes**: Per manufacturer intervals (3K-10K miles)
- **Tire rotation/balance**: Every 6 months or 6,000 miles
- **Brake inspection**: Annually or per manufacturer spec
- **Comprehensive inspection**: Annually before winter/peak season

- Check

## 7. TERM & RENEWAL

### 7.1 Initial Term
- **Duration**: 12 months from Effective Date
- **Auto-renewal**: Automatically renews for successive 12-month terms unless either party provides 60-day written notice of non-renewal

### 7.2 Performance Review
- **Quarterly check-ins**: Review fleet health, cost savings, service quality
- **Annual audit**: Full fleet compliance review, cost projection, optimization recommendations
- **Success metrics**: Target 30% cost savings, 98%+ on-time service, <2% emergency breakdowns

### 7.3 Price Adjustment
- **Annual CPI adjustment**: Up to 3% annual increase (capped)
- **Volume changes**: Renegotiated if fleet size changes >20%
