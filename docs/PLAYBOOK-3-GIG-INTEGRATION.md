# PLAYBOOK 3: Gig Marketplace Integration & Optimization

## Executive Summary

**Goal**: Transform the gig job marketplace (profitengine-v5) into a higher-margin revenue stream by leveraging company-owned vehicles from fix-and-flip program.

**Current Model** (Pure contractor gigs):
- 30% take on job fees
- Contractor provides vehicle + labor
- Low barrier to entry, high contractor churn

**Optimized Model** (Company-owned fleet):
- 100% profit on jobs using company vehicles
- Only pay contractor labor ($85-120/hour)
- Higher profit margins per job
- More reliable execution (company vehicles available 24/7)

**Revenue Target**: $7,000-$12,000/month ($84K-$144K/year)

---

## 1. VEHICLE FLEET FOR GIG WORK

### 1.1 Optimal Vehicle Types for Gig Jobs

**Best Vehicles from Fix-and-Flip:**

| Vehicle Type | Ideal for | Why | Profit Multiplier |
|--------------|-----------|-----|-------------------|
| Full-size pickup truck | Hauling, junk removal, moving | High capacity, visible | 2-3x |
| Transit van | Hauling, moving, delivery | Enclosed, secure | 2-3x |
| Full-size SUV | Delivery, small moves, hauling | Spacious, professional look | 2x |
| Cargo van | Hauling, small services | Purpose-built | 2-3x |
| Work truck | Labor-intensive jobs | Professional image | 2x |

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Sedans (limited hauling capacity)
- ❌ Small cars (not suitable for labor jobs)
- ❌ Damaged vehicles (customer-facing)

### 1.2 Fleet Strategy

**Conservative Fleet**: 3-5 vehicles
- Acquired from fix-and-flip at no additional cost (vehicles you'd flip anyway)
- Keep best vehicles for gig work (not all)
- Partial fleet (supplementary to contractor vehicles)

**Target Fleet**: 5-10 vehicles
- Mix of trucks, vans, SUVs
- Available 24/7 for contractor dispatch
- Reduces bottleneck of contractor vehicle availability
- Can handle larger jobs solo

**Aggressive Fleet**: 10-15 vehicles
- Full replacement for most contractor vehicles
- Contractors become employees (paid hourly)
- 100% control over execution quality
- Requires dedicated fleet manager

**Recommendation for Year 1**: Start with 3-5 vehicles, scale to 5-10 by Month 6

### 1.3 Fleet Maintenance & Operations

**Maintenance Responsibility**:
- You maintain vehicles (use your mechanics from institutional partnerships)
- Contractors don't pay for maintenance
- Reduces total cost of job delivery

**Fuel Responsibility**:
- Company pays for fuel (estimate $3/mile)
- Deducted from job profit

**Insurance**:
- Commercial auto insurance (not personal)
- Covers contractor + customers
- Cost: ~$1,200-$2,000/vehicle/year

**Tracking**:
- GPS on all vehicles (Samsara, Verizon Connect, etc.)
- Mileage tracking for maintenance
- Proof of location for jobs

**Formula**:
```

Your profit (pure):               $850

vs. Old model (30% take):
```

## 3. CONTRACTOR MODEL WITH COMPANY VEHICLES

### 3.1 Two-Tier Contractor System

**Tier 1: Flexible Contractors** (No company vehicle)
- Take 70% of job fee (you keep 30%)
- Use own vehicle
- Ideal for: small jobs, service-light work
- Commitment: Flexible (on-call)

**Tier 2: Dedicated Contractors** (With company vehicle)
- Paid hourly ($85-120/hour) + bonus system
- Use company vehicle (you provide)
- Ideal for: hauling, moves, heavy work
- Commitment: Full-time or near-full-time

### 3.2 Contractor Agreement for Company Vehicles

**Key Terms**:

```
VEHICLE ASSIGNMENT

The contractor is assigned a [make/model] for exclusive use
during scheduled work hours.

COMPENSATION
- Hourly wage: $85/hour (base)
- Bonus: $50-100 per 5-star job review
- Target earnings: $2,500-$3,500/month
- You earn: $2,000-$3,000/month profit per contractor

VEHICLE RESPONSIBILITIES
- Keep vehicle clean
- Report maintenance needs immediately
- Document mileage at start/end of shift
- No personal use
- Return vehicle at end of shift

INSURANCE
- Company provides insurance
- Contractor is additional insured
- Company covers all damage claims

JOB ASSIGNMENT
- Company assigns jobs based on availability
- Contractor expected to accept 80%+ of offers
- Flexibility for personal time (with notice)
```

### 3.3 Scaling Contractors with Vehicles

**Month 1-2: Testing**
- Assign 1 vehicle to highest-performing contractor
- Monitor: Vehicle condition, job quality, acceptance rate
- Target: $2,000/month profit from this vehicle

**Month 3-4: Expansion**
- Add 2-3 more vehicles
- Hire dedicated contractors (via job boards)
- Target: $6,000-$8,000/month profit total

**Month 5-6: Optimization**
- Full fleet (5-10 vehicles) operational
- Contractors becoming employees (vs. 1099)
- Target: $10,000+/month profit

## 5. JOB EXECUTION WITH COMPANY VEHICLES

### 5.1 Standard Job Flow

**Customer Submits Job** (via website or phone)
```
Customer: "I need my garage cleaned out"

You collect:
- Address
- Scope of work (size, type of items)
- Timeline (ASAP, specific date)
- Budget expectation
```

**You Quote Job**
```
Based on scope:
- Est. time: 4 hours
- Est. vehicle needed: 1 truck
- Market rate: $800-$1,000
- You quote: $900 (market)

Your profit (with company vehicle):
- Customer pays: $900
- Contractor paid: $85/hr × 4 = $340
- Vehicle cost: $50 (fuel/wear)
- Your profit: $510 (57% margin)
```

**Assign Contractor + Vehicle**
```
You: "I have Tony available tomorrow with a pickup truck. 
Can you do 10am-2pm?"

Contractor: "Yes, I'll be there"

Vehicle assigned: 2015 Ford F-150
Address uploaded to vehicle GPS
```

**Execute Job**
```
Contractor picks up vehicle
Drives to address
Executes work (customer supervises)
Vehicle GPS tracks location
Photos taken (before/after)
Customer pays (cash or app)
```

**Review & Follow-up**
```
Customer rates job (1-5 stars)
You add bonus if 5-star ($50)
Contractor returns vehicle (log mileage)
You schedule maintenance if needed
```

### 5.2 Job Quality Standards

**Contractor Checklist** (for every job):
- [ ] Professional appearance (uniform, clean)
- [ ] Vehicle clean at pickup
- [ ] Arrive on time
- [ ] Confirm scope with customer at start
- [ ] Work efficiently
- [ ] Take before/after photos
- [ ] Clean up site after
- [ ] Return vehicle clean
- [ ] Report any issues to company

**You Verify** (spot checks):
- [ ] Customer satisfaction (call after job)
- [ ] Vehicle condition (when returned)
- [ ] GPS tracking shows job completion
- [ ] Photos match customer expectations

## 7. SCALING BEYOND 5-10 VEHICLES

### 7.1 Growing to 10-20 Vehicles

**Bottleneck**: Dispatcher coordination (you can't manage all jobs)

**Solution**: Hire a dispatcher/operations manager
- Role: Assigns jobs, manages contractors, handles customer calls
- Cost: $35K-40K/year
- Frees you to focus on: Institutional partnerships, strategy
- ROI: At 15-20 vehicles, you make $1,000+/month more than their salary

**Contractor Expansion**:
- 10-20 vehicles = 10-15 contractors
- Mix of: Full-time dedicated, part-time flexible
- Consider: Employment vs. 1099 (employment = benefits, stability)

### 7.2 New Job Categories

**Once you have fleet scale**:

**Delivery Services**:
- Partner with e-commerce companies (Amazon Flex alternative)
- Same vehicles, different revenue model
- Add $2K-$4K/month

**Snow Removal** (Seasonal, Arizona limited but winter capability):
- Offer in winter months
- Use trucks with plow attachment
- Peak demand: December-February

**Landscaping Haul-Away**:
- Partner with landscapers (they generate debris)
- You dispose
- Recurring relationship

## 8. VEHICLE COST ANALYSIS

### 8.1 Annual Cost Per Vehicle

```
Depreciation (fix-and-flip vehicles have minimal depreciation):
- You flip vehicle, then keep it for gig work
- Salvage value at end: $2,000-$3,000
- Annual depreciation: $200-$300

Fuel:
- Avg 3 miles/dollar
- Est. usage: 30,000 miles/year
- Cost: $10,000/year

Insurance (commercial auto):
- $100-150/month
- Cost: $1,200-$1,800/year

Maintenance:
- Oil changes, tires, repairs
- Cost: $1,500-$2,000/year

Total Annual Cost Per Vehicle: $12,900-$15,400

Monthly Cost Per Vehicle: $1,075-$1,283

BUT: If vehicle completes 20-30 jobs/month at $500+ profit:
- Monthly profit: $10,000-$15,000
- Less vehicle cost: $1,100
- NET profit per vehicle: $8,900-$13,900/month

ROI: 7-12x (extremely profitable)
```

### 8.2 Working Capital for Fleet

```
3 vehicles (startup):
- Per vehicle cost: $1,100/month
- Contingency (1 month): $3,300
- Fuel float: $2,000
- Insurance prepay: $1,800
─────────────────────────────
Total startup: ~$7,100

5-10 vehicles (scaling):
- Per vehicle cost: $1,100/month × 7 = $7,700
- Contingency: $7,700
- Fuel float: $5,000
- Insurance prepay: $3,600
─────────────────────────────
Total scaling: ~$23,600

(But profits cover this quickly)
```
