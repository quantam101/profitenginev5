## EBOOK 3: VEHICLE RECOVERY & FLIP GUIDE ($57)

### The Complete Car Flipping System

**Step 1: Finding Cheap Cars** (10 pages)

**Craigslist Strategy**:
- Search: "mechanic", "fixer", "runs but", "parts", "$300-800"
- Call 20 listings, visit 5, buy 1-2
- Script: "Hey, what's the lowest price you'd take cash today?"
- Red flags: Title issues, flood damage, frame damage

**Estate Sales**:
- Facebook groups: "[City] estate sales"
- Newspapers (estates section)
- Contact executors directly
- Price: Usually 60-70% of market value
- Negotiation: "I'll take it off your hands this week"

**Auctions**:
- Copart.com (insurance salvage)
- Gov.deals.com (government surplus)
- Police auctions (local listings)
- Start bidding at 40-50% of retail

**Impound Lots**:
- Local police/city impound
- Usually $300-800
- Storage fees create urgency
- Call and ask: "What vehicles are available?"

**Dealer Wholesalers**:
- Build relationship with local dealers
- They get trade-ins they can't sell
- Offer cash, move inventory
- $600-1,200 typical range
