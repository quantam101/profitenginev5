## 7. Content Format Strategy

### Format Performance Expectations

| Format | Reach | Engagement | Save Rate | Best For |
|--------|-------|------------|-----------|----------|
| Single Post | 1x | 5-8% | 5-10% | Quick tips |
| Carousel (5-7 slides) | 4x | 10-15% | 20-30% | Deep dives |
| Reel (30-60 sec) | 2-3x | 8-12% | 10-15% | Hook-driven |
| Story (24h) | 2x | 15-25% (swipe) | N/A | Daily engagement |
| Video (1-2 min) | 2x | 8-10% | 15-20% | Explainers |

**Implication**: Prioritize **carousels** (4-5 per week) for reach, supplement with reels for algorithm boost.
