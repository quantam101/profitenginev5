## 💡 KEY PRINCIPLES FOR SUCCESS

### 1. Start Imperfect
Launch Week 1 with:
- Blog that's not perfectly designed ✅
- Ebooks that aren't professionally laid out (yet) ✅
- Videos that are phone-quality ✅
- Email sequences that are conversational ✅

**Perfect is the enemy of revenue.** Ship Week 1, iterate Week 2+.

### 2. Every Content Piece Has a CTA
- Blog post → Email signup link
- YouTube video → "Subscribe + check description for course"
- Email → Link to ebook or course
- No content without conversion

### 3. Track Everything from Day 1
- Google Analytics on blog
- Stripe dashboard (refresh daily)
- ConvertKit subscriber count
- Weekly revenue report

Metrics = motivation = optimization

### 4. Focus on Core 3 Products
- **Month 1**: Blog + Ebooks + Email (40% of effort, 60% of Month 1 revenue)
- **Month 2**: Add first course + YouTube (40% of effort, 40% of Month 2 revenue)
- **Month 3**: Add 2nd course + SaaS MVP (40% of effort, 20% of Month 3 revenue)

Distribution beats perfection. Launch fast, add features later.

### 5. Institutional Sales = Recurring Base
- Content revenue is important (scales fast)
- Institutional contracts = stability (predictable monthly revenue)
- Gig work = variable but high-margin (fills gaps)
- Together = sustainable business
