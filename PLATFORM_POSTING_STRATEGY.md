## Platform Analysis & Optimal Posting Times

### 🎵 TIKTOK
**Platform Stats:**
- Users: 1.5B+ globally
- Avg session: 52 minutes/day
- Content decay: Fast (3-6 hours for virality)
- Algorithm: FYP-based (no follower dependency)

**Optimal Posting Times:**
| Time Zone | Peak Times | Days | Engagement |
|-----------|-----------|------|------------|
| **US/EST** | 6-9 AM, 12-2 PM, 7-11 PM | Tue-Thu | 40-60% higher |
| **US/PST** | 8-11 AM, 1-3 PM, 8-11 PM | Wed-Fri | 35-55% higher |
| **UK/GMT** | 12-3 PM, 6-10 PM | Mon-Wed | 45-65% higher |
| **EU/CET** | 1-4 PM, 7-10 PM | Tue-Thu | 40-60% higher |

**Best Days:** Tuesday-Thursday (mid-week sweet spot)  
**Best Hours:** 6-9 AM, 12-2 PM, 7-11 PM  
**Avoid:** 2-6 AM (lowest engagement)

**Key Metrics to Track:**
- Views (first 24h critical)
- Completion rate (% who watch to end)
- Likes, comments, shares
- Watch time
- Profile visits from video
- Follower gain

**Engagement Benchmarks:**
- Nano accounts (<1K): 15-25% engagement rate
- Micro (1K-10K): 8-15% engagement rate
- Mid-tier (10K-100K): 3-8% engagement rate
- Macro (100K+): 1-3% engagement rate

**Strategy:**
- Post 3-5 times daily at peak times
- Vary content format (trending sounds, transitions)
- Post Tue-Thu for maximum reach
- Monitor FYP placement hourly
