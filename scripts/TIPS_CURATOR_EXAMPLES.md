## Tips by Performance Tier

### Top Tier (10%+ engagement)
- AI Prompts (easy to share, actionable)
- Unusual metrics/data (surprising stats)
- Contrarian takes (goes against conventional wisdom)
- Money-related tips (ROI, savings)

### Mid Tier (5-10% engagement)
- Tool recommendations (if affiliate)
- Productivity hacks (practical, implementable)
- Quick wins (results in < 1 hour)

### Lower Tier (< 5% engagement)
- General advice (too vague)
- Long tips (> 30 second read)
- No monetization (less incentive to share)

**Takeaway:** Use these examples to optimize your tip writing!
