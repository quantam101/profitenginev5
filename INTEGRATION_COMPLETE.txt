================================================================================
INTEGRATION MECHANICS
================================================================================

VOLATILITY SCALAR (Position Sizing)
  Formula: scalar = baseline_volatility / current_volatility
  Reference: 2% (configurable via baseline_volatility)
  Range: 0.1 to 1.5
  Effect:
    - < 1.0: Reduce positions (high volatility)
    - = 1.0: Baseline sizing
    - > 1.0: Increase positions (low volatility)

LOSS LIMIT ADJUSTMENT (Stop-Loss Tightening)
  Base: baseline_daily_loss_limit * regime_loss_limit_scalar
  Tightening:
    - 0-50% util: No adjustment
    - 50-75% util: -10% reduction
    - 75-90% util: -25% reduction
    - 90%+ util: -50% reduction (hard stop)

MARKET REGIMES
  LOW_VOLATILITY    (vol <1.5%):  Kelly 0.08, Loss scalar 1.3
  NORMAL            (vol 1.5-3%): Kelly 0.05, Loss scalar 1.0
  HIGH_VOLATILITY   (vol 3-6%):   Kelly 0.02, Loss scalar 0.7
  CRISIS            (vol >6%):    Kelly 0.005, Loss scalar 0.3
