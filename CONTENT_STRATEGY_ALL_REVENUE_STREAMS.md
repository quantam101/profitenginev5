## Cross-Stream Content Themes

### Theme 1: "Build Proof. Sell the Work. Then Sell the Engine."
- Used across ALL channels
- Emphasizes that ProfitEngine philosophy applies to each revenue stream
- Differentiates from "build once, sell forever" SaaS narratives

### Theme 2: "Zero Cost to Start, Real Revenue First"
- Every stream starts with no-cost setup or minimal downside risk
- Contrasts with paid software → then profit model
- Example: "Try POD for $29.99. Make $300 in 30 days. Then scale."

### Theme 3: "Affiliate Revenue: The Hidden 8th Stream"
- Every revenue stream includes affiliate routing
- Amazon Associates, Printify, Etsy, Stripe all have affiliate programs
- Hidden revenue: $500-1000/mo from affiliate traffic across all streams
