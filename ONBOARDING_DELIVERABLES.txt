VALIDATION & CONSTRAINTS
================================================================================

Email Validation:
- Format: Must match /^[^\s@]+@[^\s@]+\.[^\s@]+$/
- Uniqueness: Must not already exist in database
- Required: Cannot be empty

Password Validation:
- Length: Minimum 8 characters
- Uppercase: At least 1 letter (A-Z)
- Lowercase: At least 1 letter (a-z)
- Numbers: At least 1 digit (0-9)
- No special characters required (but allowed)

Capital Amount:
- Type: Number
- Minimum: $1,000
- Purpose: Paper trading simulation capital
- Currency: USD

Risk Preference:
- Conservative: 1-2% per trade
- Moderate: 2-4% per trade
- Aggressive: 4-6% per trade

Age:
- Minimum: 18 years old
- Validation: User must confirm age

Terms:
- Must agree to agree to complete signup
- Link to Terms of Service
- Link to Privacy Policy
